the `Allowed` section of the approved Process being performed, narrowed by the
named work — so this section states *eligibility and limits*, and never
restates Process effect policy.

second home for permissions that already live in the Process. The two copies
drift, and the Role's copy is the one nobody updates. If you find yourself
listing effects here, the effect belongs in the Process.
