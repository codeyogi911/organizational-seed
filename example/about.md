# Juniper Street Bakery

A two-person bakery on a quiet street. Bread and pastries every morning, and
custom cakes to order, about six a week. This is an invented business, here
to show the format.

## Who's who

- **Mira** bakes and owns the recipes.
- **Tom** runs the counter, takes orders and buys supplies.

Both are owners. Either one can say yes to a change in how work is done.

## What matters

- Every cake leaves on time and is safe for the people eating it.
- A custom cake is never baked without a deposit.
- Keep it small: two people, one oven, no delivery.
