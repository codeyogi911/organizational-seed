# Take a custom cake order

**Done when** the order slip is pinned on the board with everything Mira
needs to bake, and the deposit is paid.

## Steps

1. Agree the pickup day and time. We need at least three days' notice.
2. Agree the size, flavour, filling and any writing on top.
3. Ask: "Is anyone eating this cake allergic to anything, or avoiding
   anything?" Write the answer on the slip, even if it is "no". If the
   answer is nuts, don't offer almond sponge or marzipan.
   (From [this lesson](../../lessons/2026-03-15-ask-about-allergies-when-ordering.md).)
4. Take half the price as a deposit
   ([why](../../decisions/deposit-for-custom-cakes.md)).
5. Fill in the order slip and pin it on the board by pickup day.
6. The day before pickup, Mira checks the slip against what she is about to
   bake.
