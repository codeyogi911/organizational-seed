# Birthday cake with almond sponge, collected Saturday 14 March

**Asked.** On 10 March a parent ordered a two-layer almond sponge cake with
raspberry filling and "Happy 7th" on top, for pickup Saturday at 10:00. Tom
took the order and the deposit
([as usual](../processes/take-a-custom-cake-order.md)).

**Done.** Mira baked it on Friday.

**What happened.** At pickup the parent said one child at the party could
not eat nuts. The cake could not go. Mira baked a plain vanilla sponge with
the same filling and writing, ready at 12:00. The party started at 14:00, so
it arrived in time. We charged the original price and kept the almond cake
for the counter.

**Cost.** About two hours of Mira's time and one cake's ingredients. The
customer was happy in the end.

**Learned.** We never asked about allergies. Written up as a
[lesson](../lessons/2026-03-15-ask-about-allergies-when-ordering.md).
