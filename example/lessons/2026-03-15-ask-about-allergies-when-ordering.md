# Ask about allergies when taking a cake order

**What happened.** A birthday cake was ordered with an almond sponge. At
pickup the parent mentioned that one child at the party could not eat nuts.
Nobody had asked when the order was taken. Mira baked a plain vanilla cake in
two hours and we gave it at the same price
([work note](../work/2026-03-14-birthday-cake-almond-sponge.md)).

**What it teaches.** Ask about allergies at the moment the order is taken,
and write the answer down, even when it is "no". The parent knew; we just
didn't ask.

**Where it belongs.** [Take a custom cake order](../skills/take-a-custom-cake-order/SKILL.md),
as a step between agreeing the flavour and taking the deposit.

**Folded in.** On 2026-03-18 the assistant suggested the new step and Mira
said yes. It is now step 3 of the skill.
